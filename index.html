How this measurement works

Each sample navigates a fresh 640 × 360 iframe. Only one engine runs at a time; order rotates each round. All use WebGL, antialiasing, resolution 1, the same atlas and a 60°/second rotation. Bare retains its standard production build and initial-asset preloading; PixiJS and Phaser use their native atlas loaders.

Time includes document and script loading, JavaScript evaluation, engine initialization, image loading and the first render submission. It does not measure GPU completion or physical screen presentation. Browser caching is left enabled: first visits may be cold, subsequent samples are warm. This is not a controlled cold-cache test. Keep this tab visible and avoid other heavy workloads.

Results depend on your browser, hardware and cache. Render throughput and bundle-size comparisons will be separate benchmarks.
